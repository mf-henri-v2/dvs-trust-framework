## 0. Version and certification validity notes
<a id="section-0"></a>

0.a. This 1.0 publication of the UK digital verification services trust framework (‘trust framework’) will come into force under section 28 of the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 on the date the first conformity assessment body (‘CAB’) is accredited to certify against it, which will be no earlier than 1 September 2026.

0.b. The non-statutory gamma (0.4) version of the trust framework, published on 26 June 2025 and the statutory gamma (0.4) published on 1 December 2025 will remain valid for a limited period of time for the purposes of:

- certification, such that certificates issued against those versions will remain valid and any audit and surveillance in respect of those certificates are conducted against those versions as relevant, subject to 0.c below; and

- Part 2 of the Act (where relevant and where there exist certificates which are valid against them) such that references to the DVS trust framework are to be read as references to the non-statutory gamma (0.4) and the statutory gamma (0.4) versions.

0.c. The statutory gamma (0.4) version of the trust framework is only to remain valid for new certifications following the coming into force date of this 1.0 trust framework in the circumstances below:

- the DVS applied for certification against the statutory gamma (0.4) trust framework before the day on which this 1.0 trust framework comes into force but the certification process had not been completed by that date, such that a certificate was issued by an accredited CAB after that date; or

- the DVS applying for certification, at the point at which they apply for new certification, holds a certificate confirming they comply with the requirements under the non-statutory gamma (0.4) or the statutory gamma (0.4) versions of the trust framework and the application is made by that DVS before the certificate expires and within 15 months of the day on which this 1.0 trust framework comes into force.

0.d. The non-statutory gamma (0.4) and the statutory gamma (0.4) versions of trust framework referred to in 0.b will no longer apply for the purposes of certification and Part 2 of the Act, and certificates issued against them will expire and should be ignored for those purposes if any of the following apply:

- 27 months have elapsed since the day on which this 1.0 trust framework comes into force (including that day);

- a service uplifts to the 1.0 publication and has a certificate issued to confirm that; or

- the service’s non-statutory gamma (0.4) or statutory gamma (0.4) certification expires.

0.e. All previous publications of the trust framework were titled the ‘UK digital identity and attributes trust framework’. As the first revised statutory trust framework publication under the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, we have aligned the title of this publication with the term given to the trust framework in the legislation: ‘DVS trust framework’. These two titles refer to different versions of the same document.

0.f. We published a pre-release of this 1.0 publication on 3 March 2026 for business readiness purposes. We have made one change to the content of the trust framework between the pre-release and this final publication to amend 4.1.c. and 4.1.d so that a single service can be certified under the OSP role concurrently with other roles. The rule preventing this concurrent certification was new in the 1.0 pre-release, so this change will represent no practical change for OSPs uplifting their certification from gamma (0.4) to this 1.0 publication. Note that this final 1.0 publication still prevents a single service from holding concurrent CSP certification with other roles.
